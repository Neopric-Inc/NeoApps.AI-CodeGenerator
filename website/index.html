From your first idea to a working app

01

Describe your workflow

Tell your assistant what people need to do and what information the app should keep.

02

Review the plan before anything runs

Every screen, role, field and approval rule is listed in a plan you can read and change. The AI writes the plan, not the security code, and nothing is built until you approve it.

03

Publish after approval

Ask your assistant to publish the approved plan to NeoApps Cloud. Open the app link when it’s ready. You can also run the app yourself.

04

Improve the app with your team

Use the app with your team. Review its activity and ask your assistant to plan changes as your workflow evolves.

Rules the runtime enforces, not the AI

Sign-in, access and approval rules come from the plan you approved. The NeoApps runtime applies them on every request, the same way every time, including requests made through the app's AI assistant.

Access by role and by record

Clients see only their own records. Staff see what their role allows. Hidden fields stay hidden in the API, not just on screen.

Approvals with limits

Status steps, approval limits, escalation and separation of duties are checked by the server, so a manager can't approve above their limit.

Audit history

Turn on the audit log to record every change and every refused attempt: who, when, and which rule applied.

Open source and inspectable

The generator and the runtime that enforces these rules are open source on GitHub. Read the code, run the tests, host it yourself.

FAQ

Before you build your first app

Do I need to know how to code?

For the Cloud connector, describe the app in your own words and review the plan in your assistant. The local setup is available if you prefer to run the tools yourself.

Which assistants can I use?

See the assistant setup guides for supported connection paths and workspace requirements.

What will I see before I publish?

The plan describes what the app tracks, its screens, and what each person can do. Ask your assistant to revise anything that needs changing.

Does the AI decide who can see our data?

No. The AI proposes a plan; you review its roles and rules before anything is built. The open-source runtime then enforces those rules on every request. They aren't generated as code that could differ from app to app.

Do we need another AI subscription?

No. You build with the assistant your company already uses, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini. AI features inside an app use your own provider key when you turn them on.

What if I get stuck?

See the troubleshooting guide for help with connecting, signing in, and publishing.
