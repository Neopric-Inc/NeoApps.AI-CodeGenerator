FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I need to code?

No. You describe your business in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. You review a preview and approve it.

Is it a prototype or a real app?

A real app: sign-in, roles, data rules enforced on the server, and an audit trail. Teams run their business on it.

Do I lose data when I change the app?

No. Changes keep your data. If a change would delete something, you're asked first.

Who sees what?

You decide by role. Customers can see only their own records. The server enforces it, not just the screen.

What does AI cost?

Nothing from us. AI runs on your own key, so you pay your AI provider for what you use.

Can I take the code?

Yes. It's open source. Run it yourself any time.

What if I cancel?

Your apps stay available for 30 days, then they're closed with a final backup.
