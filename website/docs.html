Build your first app with NeoApps

Start with a workflow you know. This guide takes you from describing it in your assistant to opening the app your team can use.

Follow the guide Find help
First app

From idea to app link

Try the client portal with sample records. Once you’ve checked who can see and change each record, you can start working with real documents.

Choose an example

Start with a client portal or browse examples. Write down what people need to submit, who reviews it, and what they need to see.

Connect your assistant

Choose your assistant on Get Started and follow its connection steps. In a new chat, ask your assistant to use NeoApps to show your apps. An empty list is expected when you’re new.

Review the plan

Paste the starter prompt into the connected assistant. Check the proposed screens, the information they collect, and what clients and staff can do. Ask for changes and review the revised plan before approving it.

Publish and open the app

Ask your assistant to publish the approved plan and share the app link when it is ready. Open the link, follow the sign-in instructions, and test a submission and review with sample data.

Troubleshooting

What to do when you get stuck

I can’t find the connector settings

Check the steps for your assistant. A workspace administrator may need to enable connectors or give you permission to add one. If you’re working in a local coding environment, see the local quickstart.

NeoApps asks me to sign in again

Reconnect NeoApps through your assistant’s settings and use the account that owns your app. Then reopen the conversation and ask the assistant to check for the existing app or plan.

The preview needs changes

Tell the assistant which screen, field, or access rule should change. Ask it to show the revised plan before you approve publishing.

Publishing failed or I don’t have a link yet

Ask your assistant to check the status of the same publish attempt. If it failed, ask what needs to change. After you address the reported issue, ask the assistant to retry the existing app.
